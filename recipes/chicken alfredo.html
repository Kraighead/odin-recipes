Chicken Alfredo Recipe

A slow cook version of the Chicken Alfredo that we all have grown to love and is now even easier to make!

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb chicken breast
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried basil

Instructions:

  1. Cook chicken breast in slow cooker with cream and garlic.
  2. Stir in Parmesan cheese and dried basil.
  3. Serve over pasta.
